My Statement of Purpose

I stand at the intersection of logical structures and creative expression—a computer scientist who thinks like an artist, a musician who understands algorithms, a teacher who continuously learns. My purpose is to build bridges between these worlds, creating technology that feels human, art that communicates clearly, and educational experiences that empower others.

In a world increasingly shaped by digital systems, I believe we need technologists who understand more than code. We need creators who consider the human experience behind the interface, the emotional impact of design decisions, and the educational pathways that make technology accessible. My work—whether coding, designing, teaching, or creating art—aims to contribute to this vision.
